# Data Visualization Dashboards (Power BI & Tableau)

A collection of BI dashboards built to turn raw sales and performance data into decision-ready visuals.

## Tableau — Sales Dashboard by Performance & Location
Interactive Tableau workbook analyzing sales, profit, and order trends across a multi-year retail dataset (2016–2019).

**Performance view** (`Sales Dashboard by Performance.jpg`) highlights:
- Key metrics: total profit ($381K), profit ratio (13.6%), total sales ($2,801K), order count (8,834), average order value ($317), and average days to ship (4).
- Profitable vs. unprofitable customer breakdown.
- Sales by category/subcategory (Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture).
- Profit by month with category highlighting to spot seasonal trends.

**Location view** (`Sales Dashboard by Location.jpg`) highlights:
- Top 10 states by sales (California, New York, Texas, Washington, and more).
- Top-performing products by sales volume and profitability.
- A regional map breakdown (Midwest, Northeast, South, West).

**File:** `Sales Dashboard by Performance & Location.twbx` — open in Tableau Desktop/Public to interact with the live filters.

## Power BI — Product Performance Report
Report analyzing product group performance across departments (Kitchen, Garden, Clothing, Electronics, Garage) for 2019.

Highlights:
- Best and worst performing product groups by sales and year-over-year growth.
- Store breakdown by department and group.
- Year-over-year sales growth (increase vs. decrease) by department.

**Files:** `Product Performance Report.pdf` (exported view) and `Product Performance Report.pbix` (source file, open in Power BI Desktop).

## Power BI — Senior Level Profit & Loss Report
Executive-level P&L dashboard summarizing financial performance for 2019.

Highlights:
- Key KPIs: Sales ($1,387M), Gross Margin % (55%), EBIT % (16%), with YoY growth trends for sales, GM, and EBIT.
- Quarterly P&L breakdown (Sales, Cost of Sales, Gross Margin, Rent, Wages, Other Costs, EBIT).
- Target vs. actual sales tracking on a geographic map.

**Files:** `Senior Level Profit & Loss Report.pdf` (exported view) and `Senior Level Profit & Loss Report.pbix` (source file, open in Power BI Desktop).
